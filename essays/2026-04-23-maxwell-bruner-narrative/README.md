# Maxwell × Bruner — narrative psychology in *So Long, See You Tomorrow*

A long-form critical essay reading William Maxwell's *So Long, See You Tomorrow* (1980) through Jerome Bruner's narrative psychology — particularly his account of autobiographical self-making, the "subjunctive mode" of storytelling, and the folk-psychological work that narrative performs.

- **Venue**: Literary magazine
- **Target length**: 8,000 words
- **Status**: scaffolded — thesis and outline drafting
- **Started**: 2026-04-23
