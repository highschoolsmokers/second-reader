# Reading List — Maxwell × Bruner

Tiered reading list. Populated initially; run `/build-reading-list` to expand.

## Ring 1 — essential (the essay stands on these)

### Primary text

- Maxwell, William. *So Long, See You Tomorrow*. Alfred A. Knopf, 1980. (Vintage International edition also acceptable.)

### Theoretical apparatus

- Bruner, Jerome. *Acts of Meaning*. Harvard UP, 1990.
- Bruner, Jerome. *Making Stories: Law, Literature, Life*. Harvard UP, 2002.
- Bruner, Jerome. "The Narrative Construction of Reality." *Critical Inquiry* 18.1 (1991): 1–21.
- Bruner, Jerome. "Life as Narrative." *Social Research* 54.1 (1987): 11–32.

## Ring 2 — load-testing (the framework-complicators)

- Ricoeur, Paul. *Time and Narrative*, vol. 3. Trans. Kathleen Blamey and David Pellauer. U of Chicago P, 1988.
- Eakin, Paul John. *How Our Lives Become Stories: Making Selves*. Cornell UP, 1999.
- Caruth, Cathy. *Unclaimed Experience: Trauma, Narrative, and History*. Johns Hopkins UP, 1996. (to argue against)
- MacIntyre, Alasdair. *After Virtue*. 3rd ed. U of Notre Dame P, 2007, esp. ch. 15 on narrative and the unity of a life.
- Strawson, Galen. "Against Narrativity." *Ratio* 17.4 (2004): 428–452. (the anti-narrativist position)

## Ring 3 — peripheral-but-deepening

### Maxwell criticism

- Baxter, Charles. "Maxwell's *So Long, See You Tomorrow*." Introduction to Vintage edition.
- Burkhardt, Barbara A. *William Maxwell: A Literary Life*. U of Illinois P, 2005.
- Updike, John. Review / essay on Maxwell (collected in *Hugging the Shore* or *Odd Jobs*).

### Narrative theory / memory / ethics

- Phelan, James. *Living to Tell About It: A Rhetoric and Ethics of Character Narration*. Cornell UP, 2005.
- Nussbaum, Martha. *Love's Knowledge: Essays on Philosophy and Literature*. Oxford UP, 1990.

### Cultural / biographical context

- Maxwell, William. *Ancestors: A Family History*. Knopf, 1971. (companion to *So Long*)
- Interviews with Maxwell (Paris Review, "The Art of Fiction No. 71")
